Community Spaces
The heart of La REZ is found in our common areas. We have two big rooms for socialising with couches, games, free wi-fi and cable TV.

Our main common room, found in the larger of our two buildings, also has desks and a take-a-book-leave-a-book library. Throughout the day you will find residents here surfing the Net, watching the news, or just relaxing. The common room in our smaller, Erasmus building, is often the venue for lively conversations, chess games and get-togethers with munchies.

For many of us, the favourite place to hang-out is the backyard/garden area. It features rustic seating and tables, making it perfect for outdoor meals and bbq's. Relaxing in reclining lawn chairs enjoying the sun, is also a past-time here at La REZ (weather permitting, of course).

If not outside, meal times are typically spent around our two kitchens. La REZ provides refrigerator and cupboard spaces for every-one as well as some cooking supplies.





Shared Amenities
The kitchens and food preparation areas include stoves/cookers, ovens, microwaves, refrigerators, freezers and tea kettles. Although some cookware is available, residents normally use their own pots, plates and utensils. Ask the manager if you will need to bring your own. Students staying in the Erasmus house have a fully-equipped kitchen, including tableware and cutlery. 

La REZ also has a coin-operated washer and dryer available for all residents to use.

Finally, not to worry, there are enough shower rooms and toilet facilities located throughout the two buildings.
